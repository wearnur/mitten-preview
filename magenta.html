FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where do you detail?

Mitten Mobile Detailing is based in Westland and serves Livonia, Novi and the surrounding suburbs. We come to your home or work, wherever the car is parked (with the property owner's OK). Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.

Do you detail in the winter?

Yes. The winter package is done indoors, so nothing freezes, and your car still gets the full treatment when it's cold out.

What is the 3-month ceramic coating?

An affordable ceramic coating that protects your paint and keeps it glossy, with water beading right off, for about three months. A great way to keep that freshly detailed look between details.

How do I get a quote?

Send a few photos of the inside and outside of your car, plus the year, make and model. That's enough for an accurate quote.

How much does mobile detailing cost?

An interior detail is $135, and the full interior and exterior package is $175. Ask about the 3-month ceramic coating and the winter package. Call or text (248) 329-6117 with a few photos for an exact quote.

How long does a detail take?

It depends on the size of the vehicle and its condition. You'll get a time estimate with your quote.

What's the difference between a car wash and a detail?

A wash cleans the surface. A detail goes further, inside and out: seats, carpets, glass, wheels and the paint finish, done by hand.
