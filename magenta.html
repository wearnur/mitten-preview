FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where do you detail?

Anywhere in Metro Detroit. Mitten Mobile is based in Westland and comes to your home or work, wherever the car is parked (with the property owner's OK). Farther out? Just ask.

How do I get a quote?

Send a few photos of the inside and outside of your car, plus the year, make and model. That's enough for an accurate quote.

How long does a detail take?

It depends on the size of the vehicle and its condition. You'll get a time estimate with your quote.

What's the difference between a car wash and a detail?

A wash cleans the surface. A detail goes further, inside and out: seats, carpets, glass, wheels and the paint finish, done by hand.
